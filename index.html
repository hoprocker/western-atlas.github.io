Every census tract in the Mountain West, Washington, Oregon and California, scored on six criteria and grouped into 148 areas. Of 15,188 populated tracts, 10,172 pass the budget and airport filters. Missoula, Bozeman and Fort Collins lead; Seattle is seventh.

The West at a glance

  • Six criteria, equal weight. Live music; mountains and parks, with mountains counting double; peace and quiet; schools; climate risk to mid-century; walkability.
  • Scores are percentiles. Each criterion is ranked among all 15,188 tracts, 100 being best. A neighborhood’s score is the average of the six.
  • An area’s score is its three best neighborhoods. So a metro can lead on a handful of streets while most of it scores lower.
  • Two filters. A typical home value of $300k to $950k, and an airport with daily airline service within about 100 km.
  • Walkability means somewhere to walk to. Half is the EPA index; half is how close home is to a downtown, a main street or a corner of shops and cafes.

Method and limits

  • This is a first-pass screen. It uses region-wide stand-ins for the measures in the neighborhood study, so it is good for finding candidates and less good for ranking streets within a city.
  • Mountains: distance from home to terrain standing 300–500 m higher, on a 4 km elevation grid. Narrow valleys and small desert ranges are underrated.
  • Quiet: highway, rail and airport zones, the number of people living within 8 km, and average wind from airport weather stations. Station coverage is thin in Washington, Utah and Wyoming.
  • Music: a hand-built list of about 270 venues, with jazz rooms weighted higher and small-market rooms lower.
  • Climate: estimated days at or above 95°F by the 2050s, wildfire hazard, ozone and fine-particle readings for 2019–25, and dry spells. Heat estimates are rough in California’s interior valleys.
  • Town centres: a downtown needs shops and restaurants with civic offices alongside; a main street or corner needs a tight street grid or a neighborhood where at least a third of homes pre-date 1950.
  • Schools are grade levels above or below the national average for 2009–19. Colorado’s 2025 figures are estimated and Oregon has none.
  • Home value is Zillow’s neighborhood figure where one exists, otherwise the ZIP code’s. Airport distance is a straight line, so ferries and mountain passes are not accounted for except on the Olympic Peninsula.
